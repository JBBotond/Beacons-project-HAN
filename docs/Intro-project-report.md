How do you capture the attention of children in an environment filled with intricate machines, complex tools, technical language, and overwhelming visual imagery? The traditional approach of presenting complicated explanations often leads to diminished engagement and reduced enjoyment among young audiences. The client recognizes that such challenges are unnecessary and can be overcome through innovative methods. A fundamentally different approach must be implemented—one that not only captures their attention but also maintains sustained engagement throughout the learning experience. The ultimate goal remains clear: familiarizing children with the inner workings of the Bronkhorst company while ensuring that the audience stays actively engaged and focused on the subject matter at hand.
Bronkhorst’s answer to the problem is an interactive puzzle box that allows children to explore the company’s activities while maximizing engagement and aiding them in understanding complex concepts in a playful way.
This project’s main goal is to deliver a stable, educational puzzle box from KidsBytes that maximizes the practical teaching of Bronkhorst to society under the specific requirements of the client, providing a discovery-based approach to the company and increasing future visits. The project will be developed using the FRDM MXCA153 microcontroller, with the team following an iterative prototyping process and maintaining close communication with the client, who brings a commercial vision. Work will be organized in clearly defined increments—each tested and refined in consultation—and bounded by preconditions such as fixed hardware, selected components, strict cost limits, and a twelve-week project time frame.

The report is organized to transparently guide the reader through the development journey:

main functions;
